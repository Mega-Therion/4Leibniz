# Corpus licensing note

This note covers `corpus/` only. It sits outside the MIT grant in `LICENSE`,
which covers the code.

- Witness images under `corpus/latin/witness/` are pages of Gerhardt, *Die
  mathematischen Schriften*, vol. 7 (Berlin: A. Asher, 1863), reproduced from
  the public-domain Internet Archive scan
  (archive.org/details/leibnizensmathe07leibgoog). They are in the public
  domain.
- The project's own transcription and translation work (`corpus/latin/
  *_normalized.md`, `*_emended.md`, `corpus/translations/*`) is dedicated to
  the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal, so it can flow unencumbered into
  Wikisource, the Leibniz-Archiv, and any future critical edition.
- *De progressione dyadica* (LH XXXV, 3, 2) is NOT transcribed here. See
  `corpus/manuscripts/rights/de-progressione-dyadica-rights-2026-09.md` for
  the current rights analysis. The catalog records the locator and the
  reason. The ledger wins over narrative.
