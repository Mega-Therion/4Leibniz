Corpus licensing note (outside the MIT grant above):

- Witness images under corpus/latin/witness/ are pages of Gerhardt, Die
  mathematischen Schriften, vol. 7 (Berlin: A. Asher, 1863), reproduced from
  the public-domain Internet Archive scan (archive.org/details/
  leibnizensmathe07leibgoog). They are in the public domain.
- The project's own transcription and translation work (corpus/latin/
  *_normalized.md, *_emended.md, corpus/translations/*) is dedicated to the
  public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal, so it can flow unencumbered into
  Wikisource, the Leibniz-Archiv, and any future critical edition.
- De progressione dyadica (LH XXXV, 3, 2) is NOT transcribed: its first
  printing (Hochstetter/Greve/Gumin facsimile edition) remains in copyright.
  The catalog records the locator and the reason. The ledger wins over
  narrative.
