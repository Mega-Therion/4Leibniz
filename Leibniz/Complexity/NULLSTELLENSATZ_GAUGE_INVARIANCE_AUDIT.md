## 1. Algebraic Formulation of CNF Refutations

Let a Boolean CNF formula $F$ over variables $x_1, \dots, x_n \in \{0, 1\}$ be translated into an algebraic polynomial system over a ring or field $k$ (e.g., $\mathbb{Q}$ or $\mathbb{F}_2$).

1. **Boolean Domain Axioms:**
   For each variable $x_i$, enforce the Boolean idempotency axiom:
   $$b_i(x) = x_i^2 - x_i = 0$$
   Let $I_{\text{Bool}} = \langle x_1^2 - x_1, \dots, x_n^2 - x_n \rangle$ be the Boolean axiom ideal.
2. **Clause Falsification Polynomials:**
   For each clause $C \in F$, define the unique multilinear polynomial $p_C(x)$ that evaluates to $1$ when $C$ is falsified on a Boolean assignment, and $0$ when $C$ is satisfied:
   $$p_C(x) = \prod_{x_i \in C} (1 - x_i) \prod_{\neg x_j \in C} x_j$$
3. **Nullstellensatz Refutation:**
   A formula $F$ is unsatisfiable if and only if $1$ belongs to the polynomial ideal generated by the clause polynomials and the Boolean axioms:
   $$\sum_{C \in F} q_C(x) p_C(x) + \sum_{i=1}^n r_i(x) (x_i^2 - x_i) = 1$$
   The **Nullstellensatz refutation degree** is defined as:
   $$\text{deg}_{\text{NS}}(F) = \min \left\{ \max_{C \in F} \deg(q_C p_C) \;\Big|\; \sum_C q_C p_C \equiv 1 \pmod{I_{\text{Bool}}} \right\}$$

## 2. Machine-Verified Gauge Invariance Theorems

Let $g \in \{0, 1\}^n$ be a polarity mask defining the affine variable complementation:
$$\sigma_g(x)_i = \begin{cases} 1 - x_i & \text{if } g_i = 1 \\ x_i & \text{if } g_i = 0 \end{cases}$$
The transformed formula $F^g$ has literal polarities flipped wherever $g_i = 1$.

### A. Invariance of the Boolean Axiom Ideal
- **Theorem (`boolean_axiom_flip` & `boolean_axiom_mask`):**
  $$(1 - x_i)^2 - (1 - x_i) = x_i^2 - x_i$$
  *Proof:* Certified in Lean 4 via integer ring expansion ($h_1: (1-x)^2 = 1 - 2x + x^2$, $h_2: (1 - 2x + x^2) - (1-x) = x^2 - x$).
  *Consequence:* The Boolean axiom ideal is strictly invariant under $\sigma_g$:
  $$\sigma_g(I_{\text{Bool}}) = I_{\text{Bool}}$$

### B. Covariance of Clause Falsification Polynomials
- **Theorem (`litViolatedFactor_flip`):**
  For each literal factor $f(p, x) = \text{if } p \text{ then } (1 - x) \text{ else } x$:
  $$f(p \oplus g_i, \sigma_g(x)_i) = f(p, x_i)$$
  *Proof:* Certified in Lean 4 by case exhaustion on boolean polarities.
  *Consequence:* Because $p_C(x)$ is a product of literal violation factors, joint variable complementation and literal polarity flipping preserves the clause polynomials identically:
  $$p_{C^g}(\sigma_g(x)) = p_C(x)$$

### C. Degree Preservation Under Affine Substitution
- **Theorem (`refutation_degree_invariant`):**
  Since $\sigma_g: x_i \mapsto 1 - x_i$ is an affine involution of polynomial degree 1:
  $$\deg(q(\sigma_g(x))) = \deg(q(x))$$
  Substituting $\sigma_g(x)$ into any valid Nullstellensatz refutation of $F$ yields:
  $$\sum_{C \in F^g} q_C(\sigma_g(x)) p_{C^g}(x) + \sum_{i=1}^n r_i(\sigma_g(x)) (x_i^2 - x_i) = 1$$
  which is a valid Nullstellensatz refutation of $F^g$ of identical degree.
- **Main Result:**
  $$\boxed{\text{deg}_{\text{NS}}(F^g) = \text{deg}_{\text{NS}}(F)}$$
  The minimum algebraic refutation degree in both Nullstellensatz and Polynomial Calculus is a strict gauge invariant under sign reversal.

2. **The Cook–Reckhow Barrier:**

   - By the Cook–Reckhow Theorem (1979), establishing $NP \ne \text{coNP}$ (and thus $P \ne NP$) requires proving that *no* propositional proof system can have polynomial-size refutations for all tautologies.
   - Showing that algebraic refutation degree or size is superpolynomial for Nullstellensatz does not, by itself, separate $P$ from $NP$.
