As regards indivisibles, when by that word is understood simple extremities of time or of line, new extremities could not be conceived in them, nor actual nor potential parts. Thus points are neither large nor small, and there needs no leap to pass them. I am so much in favor of the actual infinite that instead of admitting that nature abhors it, as is commonly said, I hold that it affects it everywhere in order better to mark the perfections of its author.
